## 1. Description

Academic Deadline Drift Guard is intended to analyse user-supplied academic deadline information, identify differences or conflicts and propose updates for user review. It does not independently verify institutional authenticity, contact lecturers, guarantee course compliance or automatically change a calendar in the proposed v0.1 scope.

## 2. Eligibility and age

The controlled initial programme is intended for individuals aged 18 or over. It has no user account and no automated age verification. Any future service opened to minors will need an expressly approved age-appropriate design, notices and safety controls. Parents/guardians and institutions remain responsible for supervision or permissions as applicable; do not represent this as a substitute for relevant legal consent analysis.

## 3. Acceptable use and restrictions

Users must have permission to submit the material; respect other people's privacy, academic content ownership and institutional rules; verify consequential deadlines with the issuing institution; avoid uploading access credentials or other students' personal data; and not attempt to exploit, compromise, overload or use a hosted service unlawfully. The project does not write essays, submit coursework, grade students or determine an institution's rules. Users remain responsible for reviewing reports and taking actions. No one may present a proposed or disputed deadline as institution-confirmed solely because the software produced it.
