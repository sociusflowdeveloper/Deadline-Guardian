# Code of conduct

**Status: DRAFT.** Reviewed against repository source on 12 November 2026 (Asia/Singapore). Owner approval, live configuration and operational execution are not established by this document.

Contributors must communicate respectfully, avoid harassment, discrimination and threats, respect consent/privacy and never post actual student or confidential material. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an established private channel; do not publish sensitive evidence in issues. The owner assesses conflicts, may appoint an independent reviewer and may restrict or remove participation proportionately. Give affected people a fair opportunity to respond and request reconsideration privately. No guaranteed response time or employment entitlement is created. This proposed contributor policy needs owner adoption.
