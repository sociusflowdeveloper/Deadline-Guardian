# Restricted privacy, rights and safeguarding records

**Status: DRAFT.** Reviewed against repository source on 12 November 2026 (Asia/Singapore). Owner approval, live configuration and operational execution are not established by this document.

## Controlled records boundary

This repository contains instructions and non-sensitive evidence pointers only. Real data inventories, signed agreements, identities, request/incident logs and detailed security findings must live in restricted records outside Git. No restricted location, appointment, signature, consent or approval is invented. Admin must nominate the actual controlled location and accountable owners before readiness claims.

## Privacy procedure

Privacy reviewer must inventory data categories, purposes, lawful basis, source, recipients, processing locations, permissions and copies. Maintain policy, DPO designation, retention/deletion schedule, access/correction/withdrawal requests, consent/notice versions, vendor/subprocessor contracts, breach assessments/logs and test-data controls. Verify requester identity proportionately through an established private channel, assess scope/exemptions using current advice, record decision, respond safely and track completion. Use synthetic tests; never copy production/student material into fixtures. Reconcile local/provider/export/backup deletion and legal holds. Overseas transfers require destination and comparable-protection assessment; do not assume residency. Higher-risk feature changes require a privacy impact assessment.

## Rights and engagements

Admin/secretariat must obtain written confidentiality and IP ownership/licence arrangements for relevant contributors, plus vendor due diligence/agreements for paid services and data-processing terms where a provider handles personal data. Payment, a public repository or an unsigned draft is not evidence of ownership. Preserve existing licences; review third-party notices and assets before distribution. Never commit signed records.

## Younger users

Before admitting younger users assess age/capacity, intended audience, necessity, safeguarding risks, understandable notice, reporting/escalation and any parental arrangements. No universal Singapore age threshold is asserted. Current age-related proposals need explicit reconciliation and qualified advice where appropriate. Record the owner decision before real-data processing.

## Review

Admin owns open business/rights decisions, engineering owns source/testing evidence and the privacy reviewer owns data assessment. Review before new contributor, real user/data, vendor/country, contract, high-risk feature or incident, and at least annually. Unavailable records remain DRAFT requirements with evidence UNKNOWN in the register.
